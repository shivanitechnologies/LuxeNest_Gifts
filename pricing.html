Frequently Inquired Questions

Can I customize the contents of a package?

Yes! With our signature online Gift Box Builder, you can choose any box tier and hand-select individual perfumes, leather wallets, ceramics, or chocolates to suit your exact tastes and budget.

How does personalized engraving work?

Complimentary personalization is performed in our studio workshops. Leather items receive 24k gold hot foil stamping, while metal and ceramic treasures undergo laser or diamond-drag engraving.

What are the lead times for nationwide and international delivery?

Orders are meticulously packaged within 24 hours. Express courier delivers across metropolitan centers within 24 to 48 hours in shockproof climate-controlled velvet travel cases.
